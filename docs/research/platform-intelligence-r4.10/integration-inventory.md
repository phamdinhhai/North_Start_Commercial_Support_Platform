## Maturity vocabulary

Stages describe distinct evidence, not a single marketing readiness score. S0 = research claim or documented API; S1 = source-inspected model/schema; S2 = synthetic deterministic adapter tests; S3 = independently verified public provider contract fixture plus HTTP boundary tests; S4 = isolated synthetic end-to-end durable ingestion/projection; S5 = specifically authorized sandbox observation with permission/readback artifacts; S6 = approved real-data capability release. S2 does not imply S3, and S4 local delivery does not imply S5 provider delivery. Existing registry evidence labels remain unchanged.

The 39-entry `docs/research/registry-2026-11-18/provider-capability-registry.json` has 6 SYNTHETIC_TESTED, 9 SOURCE_INSPECTED, 20 DOCUMENTED_API and 4 README_CLAIM entries; runtime is synthetic for 6 and none for 33. All 39 production approvals are NOT_APPROVED. No entry is CONTRACT_TESTED, LIVE_VERIFIED or RELEASE_APPROVED. Registry validation checks schema/references, not provider feasibility.

## Five-engine alignment

| Long-term engine | Foundation contribution | Explicit boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition Intelligence | Provider/account/campaign identifiers and source touch observations | Conversation source is not acquisition lineage; ad-reported conversion is not platform outcome. Preserve timezone/currency/units/window. |
| Conversation Intelligence | Raw accepted Interaction provenance and bounded message observations | Interpretation yields draft inferred CE with confidence/evidence; provider tags are not intent/state. |
| Commercial Continuity | Stable Lead, explicit identity/context/handoff and access | No automatic Customer/Lead merge or conversation-ID lead identity (I-01, I-06, I-07). |
| Revenue Execution | Human Sales action and governed action foundations | No live outbound capability; authorization/readback/kill-switch/rollback gates remain per ADR-006/007. |
| Revenue Intelligence | Outcome claims, corroboration provenance and honest UNKNOWN | Won/order-created is not paid revenue. LVP attribution remains UNKNOWN; credit is not causation (ADR-008). |

P0 architecture target: one provider-neutral observation intake boundary, explicit source-specific validation, immutable accepted raw evidence, durable receipt/idempotency, explicit adapter mapping + policy, bounded projection and visible completeness. Metadata telemetry and research quarantines stay separate. Existing AIL and assertion outbox can inform the design; neither should be renamed to imply that target already exists. Replay must use original immutable identity/provenance and avoid duplicate assertions or privileged writes. Partial mapping must expose UNKNOWN and retain causal failure evidence without raw PII in logs.
