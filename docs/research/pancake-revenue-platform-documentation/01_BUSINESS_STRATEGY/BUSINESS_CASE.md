# Business Case

**Owner:** Founder / Strategy  
**Maturity:** Hypothesis-led  
**Evidence status:** Mixed: verified facts, user observations, inference, hypotheses  
**Last consolidated:** 2026-09-09


## Economic problem being solved
The company avoids CRM license/implementation cost but pays hidden **CRM debt** through:
- manual context transfer;
- Sales asking Marketing for history or re-contact;
- missed/late follow-up;
- duplicate and stale data;
- manager reporting effort;
- weak source-to-revenue attribution;
- leads marked unreachable despite active original channels.

## Value pools
1. Recover otherwise lost revenue.
2. Reduce Sales/Marketing coordination labor.
3. Improve contact and follow-up rates.
4. Improve Marketing allocation through lead/revenue quality feedback.
5. Reduce management reporting and reconciliation effort.
6. Delay unnecessary CRM implementation while creating a migration path to CRM later.

## Proof required before investment
Quantify current leakage, manual time, lead volume, ad spend, Sales cycle, current tool spend, and measurable uplift from an initial pilot.
