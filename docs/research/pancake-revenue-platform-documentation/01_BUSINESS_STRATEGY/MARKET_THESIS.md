# Market Thesis

**Owner:** Founder / Strategy  
**Maturity:** Draft v0.1  
**Evidence status:** Mixed: verified facts, user observations, inference, hypotheses  
**Last consolidated:** 2026-09-09


## Thesis
There is a meaningful segment of social-led SMEs in a **Stretched Pancake** state: conversation operations work well, but Sales process, handoff, follow-up, attribution, and management visibility are increasingly manual. These companies have accumulated **CRM debt** but have not yet crossed the threshold where a full CRM is worth the cost and behavior change.

## Why now
- Customer acquisition and selling increasingly cross multiple digital channels.
- Conversation data is rich but commercially unstructured.
- AI can interpret Vietnamese sales conversations and reduce manual structuring, subject to accuracy validation.
- Businesses increasingly expect measurable linkage from ad spend to revenue.

## Market boundary
Best fit is between simple chat-to-order businesses and enterprise/complex B2B organizations that already need a true CRM.

## Must validate
Market size, willingness to pay, vertical differences, API/commercial dependency on Pancake, and the real frequency/cost of handoff/follow-up leakage.
