# Business Model

**Owner:** Founder / Finance / GTM  
**Maturity:** Hypothesis-led  
**Evidence status:** Mixed: verified facts, user observations, inference, hypotheses  
**Last consolidated:** 2026-09-09


## Likely model
B2B SaaS plus implementation/methodology. A pure self-serve SaaS model is unlikely to produce trustworthy measurement early because operating definitions and handoff discipline must be configured.

## Potential revenue components
- platform subscription;
- usage/volume component for AI or conversation processing;
- onboarding / measurement-readiness implementation;
- vertical playbook / premium analytics package;
- enterprise integrations when CRM is introduced.

## Do not decide yet
Exact pricing metric, minimum contract, services margin, and packaging require interviews and pilot economics.
