# Goals and KPIs

**Owner:** Founder / Product / Data  
**Maturity:** Pre-baseline  
**Evidence status:** Mixed: verified facts, user observations, inference, hypotheses  
**Last consolidated:** 2026-09-09


## North-star candidates
- Percentage of qualified leads with preserved source + context + owner + next action.
- Percentage of qualified leads progressing to a verified commercial outcome.

## Leading product metrics
- handoff linkage coverage;
- first-contact SLA compliance;
- follow-up completion;
- commercial event acceptance/accuracy;
- original-channel recovery success;
- manager exception resolution rate;
- deterministic/inferred/unknown source coverage.

## Business outcome metrics
- qualified -> contacted conversion;
- contacted -> won conversion;
- recovered leads/revenue;
- revenue attribution coverage;
- manager reporting hours saved;
- Sales/Marketing handoff cycle time.
