# Information Architecture

**Owner:** Design / Product  
**Maturity:** Concept  
**Evidence status:** Mixed: verified facts, user observations, inference, hypotheses

## Primary surfaces
- Marketing: stay primarily in Pancake; add handoff controls/context where possible.
- Sales: `My Leads`, `Today`, Lead Detail, Timeline, Next Actions, Contact Channels.
- Manager: Command Center, Funnel, Handoff, Leakage, Data Health, Source Quality.
- Admin/Ops: Definitions, Rules, Permissions, Integrations, Attribution, Data Health.

## Lead Detail information hierarchy
1. Customer identity and original source
2. Current commercial state / owner
3. What the customer wants
4. Next action / due commitments
5. Best contact channel
6. Commercial timeline
7. Evidence / full conversation
8. Outcome / revenue
