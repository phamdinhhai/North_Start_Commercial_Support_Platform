# Sales Operating Standard

**Owner:** Sales Ops / CS  
**Maturity:** Draft  
**Evidence status:** Mixed: verified facts, user observations, inference, hypotheses  
**Last consolidated:** 2026-09-09


## Minimum required truth
- accept/own the lead;
- contact outcome;
- qualified/unqualified;
- current state;
- next action when active;
- Won/Lost and revenue/lost reason.

## Operating definitions to configure
First-contact SLA, Cannot Contact rule, follow-up cadence, Won recognition point, Lost reason taxonomy.

## Principle
Automate/infer as much as possible, but do not sacrifice business truth to eliminate every click.
