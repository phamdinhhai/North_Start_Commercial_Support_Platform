# Onboarding Playbook

**Owner:** Customer Success  
**Maturity:** Draft  
**Evidence status:** Mixed: verified facts, user observations, inference, hypotheses  
**Last consolidated:** 2026-09-09


## Day 0 - Business definitions
Agree Lead, Qualified, Won, Lost, Cannot Contact, revenue recognition, ownership, and attribution assumptions.

## Day 1 - Data/integration
Connect Pancake, map pages/users, import/sample conversations, identify current source identifiers.

## Day 2 - Process mapping
Map Marketing -> Sales handoff, follow-up, current Sheets/tools, and failure cases.

## Day 3 - Configure
Stages, SLAs, lost reasons, users/roles, handoff rules, Data Health thresholds.

## Week 1 - Pilot
Small Sales group, monitor data quality and false AI inferences.

## Week 2-4 - Business review
Compare baseline vs handoff/contact/follow-up/revenue measurement improvements.
