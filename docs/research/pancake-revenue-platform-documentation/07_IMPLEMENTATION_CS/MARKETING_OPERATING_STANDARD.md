# Marketing Operating Standard

**Owner:** Marketing Ops / CS  
**Maturity:** Draft  
**Evidence status:** Mixed: verified facts, user observations, inference, hypotheses  
**Last consolidated:** 2026-09-09


## Required discipline
- Define Lead and Qualified consistently.
- Preserve campaign/ad/source identifiers when available.
- Use a consistent handoff trigger.
- Do not hand off only a phone number; hand off a Lead ID.
- Distinguish paid, organic, returning, referral, and unknown where possible.
- Do not use naming conventions as the sole join key; preserve platform IDs.

## Quality review
Weekly review: source coverage, qualified rate, Sales contact rate, lost reasons, and source-to-revenue completeness.
