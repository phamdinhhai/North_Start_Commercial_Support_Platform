## Established learning
- Conversation operations and commercial process are different abstractions.
- The highest-potential problem is often at cross-team/system boundaries, not within a single product screen.
- Reporting gaps follow data-model boundaries: activity is easier to observe than commercial progression.
- Reliable attribution is as much an operating-model problem as a software problem.
- The strongest target may be a Yellow-zone company with visible CRM debt but low willingness for full CRM behavior change.

Every new learning should reference evidence: interview, data audit, pilot, technical test, or market observation.
