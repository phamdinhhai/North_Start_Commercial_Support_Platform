# Strategy Review Cadence

**Owner:** Founder / Product  
**Maturity:** Living  
**Evidence status:** Mixed: verified facts, user observations, inference, hypotheses  
**Last consolidated:** 2026-09-09


## Monthly early-stage review
1. Which core hypotheses changed?
2. Which ICP/vertical shows strongest pain and willingness to solve?
3. What product usage/result changed our thesis?
4. Which decisions should be reversed?
5. Are we drifting into rebuilding CRM/inbox/BI?
6. What is the next highest-value uncertainty to reduce?
