# Personas

**Owner:** Research / Design  
**Maturity:** Hypothesis-led  
**Evidence status:** Mixed: verified facts, user observations, inference, hypotheses  
**Last consolidated:** 2026-09-09


## Marketing / Inbox Operator
Owns social conversations, qualification, source context, and early response. Wants fast operation, clean handoff, and proof that Marketing produces quality revenue, not just lead volume.

## Sales Representative
Receives leads and sells via phone/Zalo/other channels. Needs context, priority, next action, and access to original engagement channel without becoming a full inbox operator.

## Sales / Revenue Manager
Needs visibility into handoff, contact, follow-up, leakage, Sales effectiveness, and commercial state without asking every rep.

## Marketing Manager
Needs campaign/ad/creative quality beyond CPL: qualified rate, Sales contact, won revenue, objections, and lead quality feedback.

## Owner / CEO
Needs a shared revenue truth: where money is spent, where funnel leaks, what revenue is at risk, and whether teams are accountable.

## Ops / Admin
Needs definitions, rules, permissions, data cleanup, imports, integrations, and measurement health.
