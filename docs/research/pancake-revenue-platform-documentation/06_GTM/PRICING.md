**Owner:** GTM / Finance

No final price is defined. Test willingness to pay against recovered revenue, avoided admin effort, existing Pancake/CRM/tool costs, and the cost of missed follow-up.

Candidate metrics: Sales users, qualified leads, conversations processed, AI usage, or ad-spend tier. Avoid pricing that recreates the exact seat-friction the product is meant to reduce without validating value.
