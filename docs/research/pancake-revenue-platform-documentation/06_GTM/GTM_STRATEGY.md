**Owner:** GTM / Founder  
**Maturity:** Hypothesis-led  
**Evidence status:** Mixed: verified facts, user observations, inference, hypotheses  
**Last consolidated:** 2026-09-09


## Beachhead motion
1. Select 1-2 verticals in the Yellow zone.
2. Sell a diagnostic: map Pancake -> Marketing -> Sales -> revenue leakage.
3. Establish a baseline of handoff, contact, follow-up, and measurement health.
4. Implement the continuity/measurement playbook plus software.
5. Prove recovered revenue or operational savings within a short pilot.

## Buyer
Likely economic buyer: Founder, Sales/Revenue Manager, or Marketing leader with clear responsibility for revenue quality.
