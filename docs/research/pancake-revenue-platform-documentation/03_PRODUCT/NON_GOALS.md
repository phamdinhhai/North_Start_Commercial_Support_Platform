# LVP Non-Goals

**Owner:** Product Lead  
**Maturity:** Active phase boundary  
**Evidence status:** Scope decision  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-10

## Do not rebuild

- Unified social inbox.
- Full enterprise CRM, ERP or POS.
- Generic task manager, BI/report builder or workflow automation.
- Generic chatbot, call transcription or enrichment platform.

## Explicitly outside the current LVP

- Public signup, billing, plan/entitlement engine and enterprise control plane.
- Production multi-tenant breadth; retain isolation seams only.
- Pancake outbound messaging.
- Meta/TikTok/Google acquisition connectors.
- Multi-touch/revenue attribution and revenue-lift claims.
- Lost-revenue prediction, forecasting and next-best-action automation.
- Automated identity merge/graph.
- Configurable stages/workflows and broad manager dashboards.
- Autonomous high-impact AI mutations.

## Revisit policy

A non-goal moves into scope only when it resolves the highest-priority learning question, has a named experiment and owner, and its prerequisites/security/API contract are ready. A feature request alone is insufficient.

The current LVP asks only:

> Can an evidence-backed handoff and action/follow-up loop create observable utility without requiring a full CRM or unverified integration?
