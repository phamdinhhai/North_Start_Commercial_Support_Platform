# Product One-Pager

**Owner:** Founder / Product Lead  
**Maturity:** Draft v0.1  
**Evidence status:** Mixed: verified facts, user observations, inference, hypotheses  
**Last consolidated:** 2026-09-09


## Working category
**Commercial Continuity / Closed-loop Revenue Platform** for social-led SMEs that use Pancake as a conversation backbone and are not yet ready for a heavy CRM, or use CRM poorly.

## Problem
The customer relationship is often broken at Marketing -> Sales handoff. Marketing captures conversation/context in Pancake, but Sales receives mostly a phone number and works via phone/Zalo. Context, original channel, commitments, and attribution are lost. Managers then lack a trustworthy view from acquisition spend to revenue.

## Product promise
Preserve customer context, channel, ownership, next action, commercial meaning, and source lineage from first conversation through Sales outcome and revenue.

## Five engines
1. Acquisition Intelligence
2. Conversation Intelligence
3. Commercial Continuity
4. Revenue Execution
5. Revenue Intelligence

## Entry wedge
The likely first wedge is not a full CRM. It is a combination of:
- relationship-rich Marketing -> Sales handoff;
- lead-scoped Sales context;
- follow-up / commitment protection;
- original-channel continuity where technically and commercially allowed;
- manager visibility across handoff and follow-up;
- CRM-lite state only where needed.

## Long-term position
Before CRM: help the company operate longer without CRM. After CRM: remain the commercial context, evidence, continuity, attribution, and intelligence layer between Pancake/acquisition sources and CRM.

## Non-goal
Do not rebuild a social inbox, enterprise CRM, ERP/POS, generic BI, generic chatbot, generic automation platform, or generic task manager.
