# Human validation protocol (proposal; NOT RUN)

Research preparation only, 2026-11-19. The user has confirmed acting as the local operator; organizational pilot approval remains pending. No participant account, invitation, consent, attendance, response or business result is implied. Do not begin recruitment or sessions until the organization approves the synthetic rehearsal, participants and observation owner.

Propose 3–5 representative participants: two Sales recipients, one handoff owner, and optionally a manager and another Sales person with different workflow experience. These are sampling roles, not new application roles or named people. Recruit only after approval, through the accountable human owner. A self-operator technical rehearsal does not count as a representative Sales study.

Use the isolated loopback synthetic environment with invented conversations and the immutable synthetic marker. Keep real customer information, provider credentials and outbound capabilities absent. Use separately authenticated existing roles; never share accounts or expand privileges for convenience. Record release revision, scenario version and flags once in the local study cover sheet. Authentication session identifiers are prohibited.

Before each session the observer explains the synthetic purpose, optional participation, the right to stop and categorical observation scope. Obtain the organization's approved consent handling; this document is not consent. No audio/video, screenshots, raw screen content or free-text participant quotations are collected by this protocol. The facilitator reads the task goal without demonstrating controls, then asks the participant to explain their understanding of evidence, claims and UNKNOWN in their own words.

For each task, start a monotonic timer when the task card is read and stop at visible completion, abandonment or intervention. Record unaided completion separately from assisted completion; an intervention ends the unaided attempt. Classify intervention as navigation, concept explanation, technical recovery or environment fault; count each separately. Preserve failed attempts and time spent. Pause timing for a declared environment outage and retain outage seconds separately. Automation durations never populate human timing fields.

Score comprehension only against the scenario rubric: distinguishes customer/lead, claim/proof, source/lineage/attribution, and recognizes UNKNOWN as missing knowledge. Use correct/partial/incorrect/not-assessed. Trust classification is appropriate reliance, overtrust, undertrust or not-assessed; record the observable category, never infer a psychological diagnosis. A claim treated as verified revenue is overtrust even if the task completed. The observer must not silently translate confusion into success.

After the task, ask whether the participant would need to check original evidence before acting and whether the claimed outcome proves revenue. Record categorical rubric judgments only. Technical failures, usability confusion and commercial understanding are separate dimensions. No acceptance percentage or time target is invented; the accountable product owner reviews observations and sets any future threshold before a confirmatory study.

Local observation files use observation.schema.json; one row per anonymous participant code and scenario attempt. Allowed codes P01–P05 are study-local and must have no identity mapping in the repository. Store completed files only in the approved private local location; never commit them. The organization must approve observation retention/deletion and access before sessions; there is no default retention period here. Delete through that approved process. Empty templates and validation rules are preparation, not collected research.

Stop the session on accidental real data, unexplained access, account mixing, participant withdrawal or blocked consent/organizational authorization. The operator records only stop categories, follows the approved incident process and does not copy the sensitive content. Business value, adoption, conversion and revenue claims remain NOT VALIDATED even after a synthetic usability study.

Validate a private completed file locally with `node product/scripts/research/validate-observation.mjs <private-file.json>`. The validator reads only, prints no values, rejects extra fields/arbitrary text and rejects an unaided-completion row with recorded interventions. It is a format guard, not de-identification proof or an approved storage/retention service.

Observation schema version 1 includes comprehensionSeconds (null when not assessed) and categorical perceived usefulness. Record human measurements only; no automated durations or invented thresholds. These observations are research records, never commercial evidence or outcome confirmation.
