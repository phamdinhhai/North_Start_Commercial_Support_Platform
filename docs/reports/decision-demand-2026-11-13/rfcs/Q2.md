# RFC Proposal Q2 — Customer, Participant, and Organization Roles

**Status:** Proposal only — segment-dependent research; not accepted.
**Owner:** Product + Data; Architecture reviews identity/cardinality changes.

## Question
Does the current Customer/CustomerRef + Lead model represent people, organizations, and per-demand roles well enough for the selected segment? If not, what is the smallest structure that avoids consequential identity, handoff, access, or result-linkage errors?

## Canonical constraints
- Customer is a person/entity and may have many Leads; Customer and Lead are never synonyms (I-01).
- Contact and Organization are not canonical first-class entities; external CRM terminology does not establish core semantics.
- Automated identity merge is deferred; links require evidence, confidence, provenance, manual correction, and reversible audit (I-06).
- Lead ID remains stable and channel/source/attribution concepts remain distinct (I-02/I-07).
- Avoid new entities and premature abstraction without evidence of an unrepresentable, consequential case.

## Alternatives
1. Keep Customer/CustomerRef and Lead; represent participant roles as evidence-backed context.
2. Add a scoped participant-role relationship only when role variation changes next action, access, or outcome.
3. Add Contact and/or Organization only if distinct identity/lifecycle/access semantics are demonstrated.
4. Keep organization structure in an external adapter where core ownership is unnecessary.

## Recommendation (proposal, not a decision)
Run a segment-specific study of multi-party decisions, shared identifiers, role changes, parent/subsidiary/branch cases, and concrete errors under the current model. Prefer scoped participant-role evidence or configuration before introducing entities. Do not alter Customer/Lead or merge automatically from vocabulary or similarity.

## Migration, security, and privacy impact
A new identity/relationship structure would affect keys, access checks, handoff projections, deduplication, audit, exports, retention, and migration of ambiguous records. Participant and organization data can be personal or commercially sensitive; minimize collection, separate identity from role claims, redact telemetry, and preserve UNKNOWN/uncertain links. No entity or merge implementation is approved.

## Required approval
Product + Data; Architecture and Security/Privacy when identity, access, or sensitive participant data changes. ID-1/ID-2 approvals remain separate where applicable.

## Expiry / review
Review when the pilot cohort includes organizational or multi-party purchasing, or by 2027-01-13. Until then current model remains unchanged.

## Unresolved questions
- Which role distinctions change operational decisions rather than merely labels?
- Is a participant a Customer identity, an external identity, or an assertion about a Lead?
- How should shared contact data and uncertain organization links be surfaced?
- What access scope is justified for each participant role?

## No-decision / no-go conditions
- **No decision:** no material case, unresolved identity ownership, or no evidence that simpler context fails.
- **No-go:** silently adding Contact/Organization as core truth, automatic merge without evidence/reversible undo, collapsing Customer and Lead, or broadening access from a participant label.
