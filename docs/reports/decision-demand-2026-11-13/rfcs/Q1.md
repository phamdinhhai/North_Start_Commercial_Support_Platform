# RFC Proposal Q1 — Cross-Module Result Linkage

**Status:** Proposal only — research/review request; not accepted.
**Owner:** Product + Data; Architecture owns reference/persistence review; Security/Privacy reviews real data.

## Question
What relationship and provenance should connect a Lead/demand episode to module-owned records such as order, enrollment, payment, refund, or renewal when cardinality, corrections, late evidence, and source authority vary?

## Canonical constraints
- Lead remains one acquisition/commercial episode with stable identity; Customer and Lead are distinct (I-01/I-07).
- Conversation source, acquisition lineage, and revenue attribution must not collapse (I-02).
- Outcomes/revenue are asserted with provenance; Won is not automatically revenue or attribution evidence.
- CRM/external modules retain their semantics behind anti-corruption boundaries (I-10/I-11).
- MM-1 remains open; current LVP excludes full revenue attribution and does not require a shared Outcome entity.

## Alternatives
1. A direct typed reference owned by the result module, with explicit cardinality, provenance, correction, and authority rules.
2. A separately governed association/lineage record referencing both module-owned records without moving semantics into a shared Outcome.
3. An adapter/evidence pointer that remains a claim until the module is integrated and authoritative.
4. A shared result abstraction only if validated workflows show stable common semantics across modules.

## Recommendation (proposal, not a decision)
Start with a separately governed association/evidence record and preserve each module's ownership. Test zero/one/multiple results, partial payment, refund, renewal, duplicate/late evidence, correction, and authority conflict before considering direct references or a shared abstraction. Keep attribution credit separate and UNKNOWN where unresolved.

## Migration, security, and privacy impact
Any accepted mapping affects identifiers, foreign-key/cardinality choices, reconciliation, correction history, exports, retention, and access scopes. Cross-module joins must use immutable external IDs, not display names. Payment/health/education and other sensitive data require purpose limitation, minimization, encryption, role/resource authorization, redaction, and deletion/legal-hold review. No cross-module schema or connector is authorized.

## Required approval
Product + Data + Architecture; Security/Privacy before real data or sensitive result fields. If attribution semantics change, a separate MM-1 approval is required.

## Expiry / review
Review when result data is permissioned and available, or by 2027-01-13. No LVP blocker unless validated demand requires the link.

## Unresolved questions
- Which system is authoritative for each result and correction?
- Can one result legitimately map to multiple Leads and how is allocation represented?
- Are direct references compatible with module release/versioning boundaries?
- What minimum evidence is sufficient to display a result linkage?

## No-decision / no-go conditions
- **No decision:** no permissioned cases, unclear authority/cardinality, or missing cross-module owner approval.
- **No-go:** hard-coded causal attribution, fabricated revenue, display-name joins, opaque cross-module writes, or bypassing source/module access controls.
